    What does an ETA leave out?

    An arrival prediction can be accurate while the rider still has a difficult journey. This meter records the conditions of the work, including an on-time delivery.

    Documented prediction inputs

    • Route, location, time and current traffic — Uber
    • Dish quantity/type, restaurant patterns and footfall — Zomato
    • Rider waiting and counter pickup time — Zomato
    • Restaurant load, geography and rider availability — Swiggy
    • Recent delivery durations and supply-demand balance — DoorDash

    Prediction is not the whole experience

    Rain and waiting are not universally ignored. Weather may affect predictions or operations; predicted waiting does not establish whether a rider is compensated. Discomfort, personal safety decisions, rest needs and net earnings are different questions. Their use as explicit ETA inputs is not established by the public sources reviewed.

    This demonstration

    Kitchen ready: 8 min
    Rider reaches restaurant: 4 min
    Restaurant → customer: 12 min
    max(4, 8) + 12 = 20 min

    This is a teaching example, not a platform algorithm. Cooking and rider arrival overlap. Rain, traffic and hazard exposure occur within the journey; they are not extra minutes added a second time.

    The delivery arrives on time. The meter still preserves four minutes waiting and the conditions the rider encountered. It does not infer stress, injury, lost pay or penalties.

    Further evidence: Zomato Weather Union · Interview study on invisible labour · Fairwork India 2024

    Public disclosures are incomplete and time-specific. No production model, sensor, GPS, platform account, pay rule or enforcement system is connected.
